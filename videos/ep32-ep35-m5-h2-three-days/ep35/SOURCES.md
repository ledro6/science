Basile Audoly and Sébastien Neukirch, *Fragmentation of Rods by Cascading Cracks: Why Spaghetti Does Not Break in Half*, Physical Review Letters **95**, 095505 (25 August 2005), DOI [10.1103/PhysRevLett.95.095505](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.95.095505).

## Claims used

1. Bent dry spaghetti often breaks into more than two pieces, typically three or four: primary abstract. The on-screen number is qualified by «часто» and is not a universal piece count.
2. Feynman was puzzled by the experiment: primary introduction, referring to his anecdote (reference 12). The narration makes no unsupported claim that he solved it, gave it a formal name, or spent a specified number of years on it.
3. The first crack creates free ends. The release of their initial curvature launches a burst of flexural waves: introduction and derivation around equations 1–4.
4. These waves locally increase curvature beyond the original uniform bending: equation 4, Figure 2, direct experiment Figure 3. This transient may cross a fracture threshold and create additional breaks.
5. Timing and location of failure vary; initial curvature and defects matter: experimental discussion after Figure 3. Therefore the script explicitly says another break is not guaranteed.
6. The 2005 work is the mechanism described in the episode, with both authors credited. «Парадокс спагетти» is a popular topic label, not a mathematical paradox.

## Visual model and limitations

`pasta-geometry.mjs` computes the self-similar early-time shape using κ(s,t)=2κ₀ S(s/√(2πγt)), then integrates the tangent from a fixed outer end. A discrete lookup evaluates the Fresnel sine integral. Geometry, dimensions and display time are normalized; no material measurements or precise crack position are asserted. The model is valid for the explanatory early transient before reflected waves dominate; it is not a complete fracture solver. The additional fracture and subsequent detached-piece motion are deliberately illustrative. The schematic label remains on explanatory scenes.

The narration avoids implying that a uniformly travelling transverse pulse, rigid recoil, or reflection alone is the complete mechanism. No 2018 twisting method is introduced. No stock video, real lab footage or user-shot experiment is used or claimed.
